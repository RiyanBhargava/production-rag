TRADING PLATFORM CUSTOMER OPERATIONS
Fictional internal procedures for a software demo. These are not real company policies.

STANDARD WITHDRAWAL
A standard withdrawal is reviewed within 24 hours after account verification.
Support checks the account verification status and the withdrawal reference.
If review exceeds 24 hours, support escalates the case to payments operations.

SECURITY-FLAGGED WITHDRAWAL
A security-flagged withdrawal remains on hold until the security team clears it.
Support must not release the withdrawal or bypass account verification.
Support escalates the case to security operations and gives the customer a status
update without revealing internal fraud-detection rules.

CUSTOMER COMMUNICATION
Support must acknowledge a withdrawal complaint within 15 minutes.
Customer urgency does not override a security hold or verification requirements.
