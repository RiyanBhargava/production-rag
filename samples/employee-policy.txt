# Employee policy

## Annual leave
Employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave each year. Leave requests must be submitted 10 business days before the start date.

## Device security
Report lost company laptops to the security team within 2 hours. Error code SEC-401 means the device certificate has expired.

## Expenses
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days. Claims above 500 AED need manager approval.
